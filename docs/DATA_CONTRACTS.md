# Tactical contracts — schema version 2

`Battle.SchemaVersion=2`. The default roster and unit statistics come from validated `scenarios/skirmish.v2.json`; the same embedded data is used by headless tests and the game. Unknown fields, numeric/unknown enum values, unsupported schema/terrain, duplicate IDs, invalid deployment and out-of-bounds unit values fail. Supported authored terrain names: `ridge` and `flat` (flat is useful for mechanic fixtures). Four unit kinds derive Sword/Spear/Bow/Lance weapons, with configurable speeds/radius/power/armor/range/ammunition/discipline. This is not a save-game or campaign schema.

IDs are positive integers scoped to the battle. Factions 1 and 2 have analogous mechanics; the commander controls faction 2 only. State starts with strength 24 per default formation (288 troops total), ammo 30 formation salvos for archers, morale 900, cohesion 1000, fatigue 0. Morale/cohesion/fatigue use 0..1000 scales. Facing is 0..3599 tenths of degrees: north (-Z)=0, east=900. Positions are X/Z millimetres within a 100m square, subject to footprint clearance.

`TacticalOrder` records version, increasing battle-local ID, future delivery tick, owning faction/formation, Move/Attack/Hold/Withdraw/Rally, target, optional observed enemy ID, append flag and common group speed cap. Queueing and receiving are separate. Normal movement replaces active/queued destinations; append adds waypoints. Group validity is checked before any orders are enqueued, with clear errors for invalid ownership, routing units, destination footprint or non-observed attack target. A path can still fail after receipt, reported as NoPath; no unit teleports through a blocked route. Post-victory requests are rejected.

`EnemyObservation` stores ID/type/last seen position/strength/facing/radius/status, observation tick, confidence and current-visibility flag. No live enemy object or order is included. Contacts stale without updating hidden positions and expire after 200 ticks (10 seconds). Current contacts share instantly within the faction. Sight limit is 36m; forest targets concealed beyond 10m unless recently firing. More than 4m of forest depth, intervening high ground or rock blocks LOS. Recent fire can expose a forest-edge archer for 40 ticks but does not bypass ridge/forest-depth obstruction. Rank contact uses footprint reach and clear ground segment to reveal legitimate close encounters even in woods.

`ArmyKnowledge` contains copied owned views and perception contacts. `PlanDecision` records tick, plan, observed IDs, reason, issued commands and full copied belief snapshots. The planner cannot receive Battle/live Formation. Active HUD excludes its decisions; brief plan names appear after battle. Tests have access to the trace for reproducible verification.

RNG is a versioned-in-code xorshift32 stream; seed 0 maps to a nonzero internal seed. The prototype seed is 42. Fixed seed and scheduled inputs produce repeatable outcomes/events/decisions on the tested pinned runtimes across frame partitions. Integer state and stable iteration improve reproducibility; universal bitwise equivalence across architectures/runtime versions is not claimed. Movement normalization/facing use rounded Math.Sqrt/Atan2; no unvalidated cross-architecture guarantee. No save/replay persistence is implemented.
